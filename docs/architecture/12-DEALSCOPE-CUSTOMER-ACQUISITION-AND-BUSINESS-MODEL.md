## 1. The commercial company and product proposition
DealScope is the SAR-owned independent **external multi-tenant commercial intelligence and exchange suite**, not the SAR corporate website or private ResourceOS, and not a Possent Systems/TenderDesk/BidOS asset. The core public promise is **Know what you're dealing with before you commit.** The three decision layers are:
- **Integrity:** are the identity, buyer, seller, stock, title, mandate, assay and payment claims independently corroborated? Which claims are contradictory or missing? How should the user investigate?
- **Viability:** does the transaction make economic sense across grade, units, yield, source cost, transport, processing, assays, financing, margin and capital?
- **Readiness:** which documents, permissions, prices, financing, professional or commercial approvals and deliverables remain before commitment?

The source of lasting differentiation is credible evidence, versioned private opportunity data, correctly modelled commercial economics, explicitly justified concerns and an evolving opportunity decision record. A calculation or AI narrative alone is insufficient. DealScope must never guarantee criminality, legitimacy or the existence of a buyer/stockpile based only on self-supplied data.

## 2. Customer segments
1. Deal originator/intermediary — investigate whether a prospective commercial opportunity deserves time, travel, samples, funding or negotiation.
2. Commodity trader — calculate buy ceilings, logistics/process routes, financing cost, net margin and scenario sensitivity.
3. Mine or stock supplier — organise lawful parcel-specific title, selling rights, verified quality and consent-based buyer exposure.
4. Buyer/offtaker — evaluate specifically disclosed counterparty offers and associated costs/evidence without bulk buyer/seller data leakage.
5. Enterprise procurement, commercial, logistics and finance teams — maintain private team workspaces and repeatable auditable decision workflows.
Support chrome and other hard/soft commodities with industry-specific specifications and units; no chrome-only system assumptions.

## 3. Signed-out funnel and visitor journey
Entry by user-consented WhatsApp group postings, direct outreach, search/SEO, partners or referrals -> **dealscope.co.za** -> trust-centred public hero -> detailed Integrity/Viability/Readiness benefits -> illustrative report with no fabricated live case -> the full capability grid -> customer journey -> transparent trial/price options -> trial onboarding -> verified identity/organisation -> complete DealScope access -> first dossier -> source/evidence request -> Deal Run -> monitoring/reassessments -> buyer/seller permitted Exchange -> voluntary PAYG/subscription conversion.

No automatic WhatsApp scraping, group-member harvesting, disguised affiliate campaigns or public secret-bearing referral links. Use optional privacy-scoped UTM tags; independently verified opt-in for tracking where necessary.

## 4. Full product value and repeat-use architecture
The following remain IN SCOPE concurrently: DiscoveryOS/MarketOS, Deal IntegrityOS, EvidenceOS, CounterpartyOS, Opportunity/DossierOS, Deal Runs, Simulation/ScenarioOS, Watch/Alerts, Commercial Readiness, buyer/seller ExchangeOS, Communications/Negotiation, customer reporting, subscription/PAYG billing, company team administration, support, immutable audit and recovery.
The customer should return when real new evidence, a revised quote, a new buyer indication, assay expiry, processing route, funding constraint or market movement materially changes the original decision. History, comparison and investigation worklists create recurring value. Do not trigger a paid Deal Run automatically for a mere market alert.

### Marketing and upsell sequence
1. Trust: public self-check and example of credible evidence scrutiny.
2. Try: **ONE free full deal assistance & assessment cycle** for one real customer-owned opportunity, once per eligible person/organisation, without calendar-based unlimited access (per approved eligibility policy).
3. Apply: create actual dossiers, assessments, alerts, scenarios, and permissioned Exchange activities.
4. Pay by choice: one-off PAYG report, Deal Run credit packs, Essential/Professional subscriptions, and distinctly priced permitted Exchange / professional verification services where approved.
5. Expand teams with corporate seats and management workflows after a clear payment agreement.
Paid analysis may conclude "not financially viable" or "insufficient evidence"; fulfilment is judged by delivering the disclosed service with appropriate quality, not by successful execution of the original deal.

### Historical internal price baseline — NOT publication approval
Earlier proposed or assistant-referenced pricing (not independently ratified as current tariffs): 1 PAYG Deal Run R450; 5-pack R1,500; 15-pack R3,500; Essential R15,000/year for 1 seat and 20 runs/month; Professional R28,500/year for 3 seats and 50 runs/month. These amounts require founder price adoption, VAT/tax treatment, provider costs, legally compliant payment terms and unit-economics/market validation. Website source therefore currently displays product categories **without binding prices**.

## 5. Measurement and commercial optimization
Measure referral visits, signed-out value/CTA engagement, consented registration, identity verification, free complete-deal assessment activation, first investigation, first complete Deal Run, verified evidence follow-up, private qualified opportunities, watch engagement, day-7/day-30 retention, full-trial-to-paid conversion, paid repeat usage, customer support, report delivery/refund, provider fees and gross contribution per customer. Any cross-tenant public promotion requires explicit seller consent/independent evidence review.
