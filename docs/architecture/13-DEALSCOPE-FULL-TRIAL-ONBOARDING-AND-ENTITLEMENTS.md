## Current commercial entitlement
The default introduction is **ONE FREE COMPLETE DEAL ASSISTANCE & ASSESSMENT CYCLE** for one eligible verified person/company/deal originator and one unique opportunity. No 30-day unlimited trial. Founder floated one or two: **one** is the approved planning default; a **second** can only be separately offered through an independently approved promotional campaign, never automatically. This is a one-deal service-volume limit, not the right to restrict the full product suite.

A completed cycle must deliver (1) deal intake and private dossier, (2) identity, mandate, stock rights, lab/sample/buyer credibility and evidence investigation with explicit uncertainties, (3) route economics, margin, acquisition ceiling and multiple scenario assessments, (4) commercial readiness and investigation action plan, (5) complete versioned retrievable Deal Assessment report. Never pretend unprovided licensed issuer, physical inspection, assay or paid professional work was actually performed. External verification costs and inclusions require clear offer terms.

## Exact consumption and non-charging rules
Reserve the free cycle against a single real dossier, not against an individual calculation. Revised rates, quotes, supporting evidence, altered scenarios and findings within the SAME parcel/counterparty-defined opportunity are part of that full cycle. Different stockpile, party, material lot or unrelated commercial contract needs a fresh deal entitlement; cannot be disguised as an amended original. Consume the cycle as completed only after all mandatory attested stages and actual delivery of a report. A negative or unverified deal can still be a properly fulfilled assessment. On actual failed service delivery, use a controlled, one-time audit-backed recredit/appropriate remedy; never debit the customer or assume completion merely because a draft exists.
There is NO default 30-day clock. Any case idle/closure/reclamation policy needs its own Founder-reviewed explicit customer terms and must never silently recreate the obsolete duration-based offer. On completion customer can voluntarily select approved PAYG, credit packs, subscriptions or permitted Exchange services; no compulsory card, automatic trial conversion, automatic paid run, success fee or fund release.

## Identity and anti-abuse
Independent verified natural-person and organisation identity, actual representative authority, active membership and source-proof enrolment; one intro claim per eligible user/entity under explicit policy, no new free deal by email alias, phone change or corporate user invitation. Protect identity correlation with lawful privacy-reviewed keyed HMAC; no raw IDs or bank details in public material. Provide legitimate customer appeal for erroneous duplicate matches. Authorised separate second-cycle campaign requires approval, named approver different from customer, campaign ID/version/expiry, individual eligibility, immutable ledger and replay controls.

## Platform completeness
The entire DealScope product remains in scope: DiscoveryOS, MarketOS, IntegrityOS, EvidenceOS, Opportunity/Viability/ScenarioOS, Readiness, Watch, buyer/seller workspaces, controlled Commercial Exchange, paid entitlements, support and audit. Free service volume does not imply trial users are given a fake demo or bypass legal/verification controls. No independently verified sources or live Exchange should be marketed as functioning until actually connected.
