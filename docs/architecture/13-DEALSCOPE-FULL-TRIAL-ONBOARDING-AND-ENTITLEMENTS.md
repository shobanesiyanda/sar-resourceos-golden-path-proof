## Product entitlement contract
1. A trial is issued to a **real authenticated, eligible natural person and/or a recorded, legitimately represented organisation**, not an anonymous cookie/browser nor a user's arbitrary typed company name. Sole deal originators can register as individual account holders represented by one user-scoped trading organisation if required by the multitenant data model.
2. The free introductory entitlement is **30 complete 24-hour days from trusted activation**. Do not start clock on a landing page visit, marketing link click, invitation without onboarding, or merely an email sent. Server records UTC timestamp, intended expiry and policy version; user sees local expiry with timezone.
3. Full feature set is the **same product capabilities**, not a read-only or artificial demonstration: sourced opportunity intelligence, private evidence, Deal Runs, scenario history, watches, buyer/seller workspaces, consent-scoped Commercial Exchange, reporting and documents. No arbitrary shortening to one free calculation. Features dependent on unprovisioned third-party real data are NOT represented as active; legal KYC, privacy, safety, merchant, buyer/seller verification and lawful contractual authority gates apply equally to paying customers.
4. Enforce trial eligibility through authoritative company and user identity proof, recorded independent approval and **unique, consistent person/org trial ledger keys**, not solely phone/email/IP, which can be changed. Never expose national ID/company proof or raw bank details as trial claim keys; use pseudonymised protected identity correlation under approved POPIA policy and retention.
5. A legitimate organisation has one introductory organisation-wide trial. Invite authorised team members into that tenant according to role policy without silently creating new organisation-wide 30-day trials for each seat. Separately eligible sole individuals may receive their own first trial in a separate tenant only after verified policy eligibility. Avoid unjustified denials from a weak common email domain or shared mobile device.
6. Eligibility recording and grant must be atomic: verify no prior person/org claim, insert unique trial-claim keys + entitlements + immutable event within a transaction; concurrent signups cannot both succeed. Rejected/duplicate attempts must not delete an earlier grant.
7. **No payment method required** for proposed zero-risk default. No charges, renewal, default subscriptions or automatic paid conversion at trial expiry. After expiry, keep security-authorised historical read/export where lawful, disable new billable operations until the user chooses a plan, preserve audit/recovery. Explicit conversion selection and exact price+VAT/terms is a separate customer authorisation, not implied by trial.
8. Use voluntary onboarding: profile role, company/sole trader choice, verification and invitation, first opportunity, evidence/self-check, trial-status dashboard, support and trial expiry reminders. Do not auto-enrol members from WhatsApp contact details.
9. Server computes entitlements; UI badge is only a reflection, never permission. Free, trial, expired, paid, suspended, refunded/disputed, revoked and enterprise roles have distinct domain states. A buyer or seller may be commercially verified only by independent policy/evidence, not by activating a trial.
10. Where expensive third-party provider verifications are requested, disclose vendor cost and consent at the point of transaction where that fee is separately payable under founder-approved terms; **never quietly turn off a core suite module for a trial**. Product-wide fair-use and anti-abuse may be applied equally under published terms; avoid claiming unlimited human services without capacity/evidence.

## Proposed journey and lifecycle
Signed-out visitor -> /start (role and product illustration) -> future /signup verified email -> identity/organisation claim -> server eligibility + prior-claim proof -> grant candidate -> **atomic activation of all normally permitted capabilities** -> initial real private opportunity -> active day 1–30 -> valid reminders optional -> expire without charge -> explicit selection PAYG/credits/annual subscription or remain read-only. Enforce same tenant permissions for all users. Provide correction and appeals for misidentification and automated eligibility false positives.
