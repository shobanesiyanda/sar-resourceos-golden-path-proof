# DealScope — Commercial Assessment Method, Customer Conduct and Evidence Disclaimer
**Control:** DS-METHOD-20261028-001 | **Status:** Controlled proposed customer trust/methodology annex; not a certification.

## Product distinction
DealScope's three independently explainable decision layers are: **Integrity** (support for identities, selling authority, documents, physical stock and payment claims), **Viability** (revenue, acquisition price ceiling, costs, grade/yield, finance, logistics, scenarios) and **Readiness** (remaining documents, permissions, financing, milestones and commercial approvals). Do not collapse them into a single fabricated "legit deal" badge or claim that a mathematically profitable candidate is independently available for execution.
## Evidence taxonomy
1. User-asserted claim: not verified, who asserted, time and claimed scope.
2. Uploaded third-party document: existence of file does NOT mean issuer/stock/mandate has been authenticated.
3. Independent source check: name, credential, licence, timestamp, source and exact limited facts confirmed.
4. Contradiction or expiry: exact conflicting item and dated source, action required and reassessment trigger.
5. Unsupported item: clearly outstanding, not falsely passed/failed.
6. Assisted professional review: identify actual reviewer, responsibility, fee, report scope and qualifications; no generated signature or simulated inspection.

## Qualification and customer assessment
Public opportunities shown as "qualified" must be current, independently source-grounded, economically positive above disclosed acceptance thresholds and actionable. Break-even, below-threshold and rejected candidates belong to restricted screening logs; do NOT list them as public profitable deals. Customers may submit their OWN questionable or uneconomic prospects for private assessment, and a negative report may be a valid paid deliverable. No guarantee of stock existence, third-party trade, license compliance, buyer payment or profit.
## Customer duties, acceptable use and restricted trade
Prohibit impersonation, false mandates, forged assays, unauthorised stock offers, fraudulent proof of funds, raw credentials, unauthorised private data uploads, scraping/exfiltration, bypassed privacy controls, ransom/illegal transaction facilitation and threatening/consequential defamation of named parties. High-risk cases must be escalated for authorised human review; the service should objectively explain discrepancies without accusing a party of criminal conduct based on missing records.
## Human approvals and cost scope
No automatic binding RFQ, sale, purchase, sampling, site inspection, contract execution, financing commitment, trade exchange disclosure or payment instruction from a model suggestion. Paid issuer, lab and on-site verification require specific customer consent and a truthful defined extra cost. Full first free Deal Assistance & Assessment is not falsely described as covering unlimited paid third-party physical services.
## Legal/operational controls
Legal reviews accuracy, disclaimers, consumer remedies, acceptable-use enforcement and liability. Security/privacy review records processor rights, source IP/licensing, buyer/seller confidentiality, correction and evidence provenance. Genuine verified providers, actor authority and live integrations are release gates.
