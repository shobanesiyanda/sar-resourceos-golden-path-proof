# DealScope — Processor Agreement Requirements and Counterparty Disclosure Consent
**Control:** DS-DPA-20261028-001 | **Status:** Draft procurement and security annex, NOT executed provider contracts.
**Data controller/responsible party:** Shobane African Resources (Pty) Ltd, DealScope business line.

## Processor contract minimum
Before any external hosting, AI inferencing, correspondence, WhatsApp messaging, analytics, identity registry, verification, assay service or PSP processing, contract must specify: parties/legal identities, subject matter, dataset categories, legitimate purpose/instructions, security/access controls, confidentiality, approved subprocessors, location and international transfers, incident notification and cooperation, retention/return/deletion, purpose limitation, audit cooperation, liability, breach handling, backups, lawful regulator disclosure and termination. No provider should independently resell customer dossiers, develop lookalike intelligence using them without lawful authority, or reuse buyer/seller contact lists.

## Financial processor boundary
Card and payment data are handled by the separately licensed and contracted SAR merchant provider; DealScope must not store raw CVV or card numbers. Capture/refund signatures and merchant identity must be independently checked, with no financial action from a browser redirect alone.

## Buyer/seller Exchange consent
Consent must capture the exact opportunity/dossier revision, data categories, who is authorised to disclose, named intended recipient/business, permitted purpose, expiry/revocation, lawful seller/material rights, identity and action/time. Disclosures are per recipient rather than broad website indexing or an undefined "all buyers" access. Customer may view what was shared, to whom and when; a grant cannot elevate another tenant's access to unrelated dossiers. Suspended/unverified counterparties cannot receive privileged records. Record remedies and lawful revocation implications for previously delivered information.

## Parent business separation
SAR's common legal ownership does NOT grant unrestricted access to DealScope customer opportunity documents in ResourceOS trading. Require distinct commercial authority, customer consent or other lawful basis, named staff role and immutable audit for any otherwise legitimate cross-business use.
