# DealScope — Payments, Cancellations, Refunds and Subscriptions (CONTROLLED DRAFT)
**Version:** DS-PAYMENTS-20261028-001 | Draft / no payments operational.
**Supplier/merchant beneficiary:** Shobane African Resources (Pty) Ltd, using the DealScope brand. Actual licensed provider and merchant registration still unverified.

## 1. No-charge onboarding
One eligible person/company receives ONE full end-to-end deal assistance and assessment on one defined opportunity: intake, integrity investigation guidance, route/viability scenarios, readiness and complete issued report. This is not a 30-day unlimited-use period or fee-free external assay/verification service. A second free deal only by separately approved and recorded campaign. No bank/card details required for the free offer; never auto-enrol in a paid subscription or charge when the report finishes. A genuinely undelivered assessment requires documented complaint and restoration/re-performance remedy. A negative but adequately investigated transaction assessment still meets the ordered service.

## 2. Price transparency and order formation
The platform must show the exact approved service version, quantities, full VAT-inclusive ZAR price and separately consented provider charges, cancellation rights, fulfilment period, transaction terms and SAR supplier details **before** final placement. Offer review/correction/withdrawal, and send a durable customer order record/receipt. Past proposal prices are NOT public tariffs. A draft screen, clicked CTA or unverified payment-success redirect must not trigger an entitlement. No "free" offer with hidden checkout, paid assay, surprise card hold, shipping fee or compulsory additional verification.

## 3. Approved payment mechanism
When ready, licensed SAR-authorised PSP securely processes embedded web or native checkout where actually supported and permitted. Never store raw payment card numbers, CVV, payment passwords or unverified bank details in DealScope. Verify signed/correlated provider events server side, order, real captured amount, ZAR currency, actual SAR merchant ID, unique webhook key, refund/original transaction and correct customer organisation before atomic credit grant. No reliance on screenshots or browser redirect for capture. Finance maintains reconcilable merchant-to-bank settlement, fees and disputes; failed provider events do not create revenue or active paid features.

## 4. PAYG credits and service completion
Credit packs/Deal Runs buy defined commercial assessment output, not contract success, confirmed stock/title, guaranteed buyer or revenue. Reserve a credit only after customer authorises a distinct analysis run; consume on completed deliverable, with a failure/recredit mechanism where service not delivered. Cross-org credits, double posts, duplicate refunds and unsolicited upsells are blocked. A re-run to correct supplier's proven mistake must not be silently charged as a second service. Paid external providers are separate order lines with actual authorised cost disclosure.

## 5. Recurring subscriptions — only after approved terms
No automatic paid trial conversion. A paid subscription requires affirmative order and accepted price, billing period, term/renewal terms, accessible cancellation method and exact customer notice/cooling-off rights. Do not advertise auto-renewal/commitment benefits before backend and consumer-law review. Where CPA s14 applies, follow applicable fixed-term cancellation and notice requirements; it expressly excludes transactions between juristic persons in its own terms, which does not remove all other laws. Retain easy access to paid invoices, cancelled subscription receipts and entitlement status.

## 6. Consumer cancellation, cooling-off and refunds
Rights are determined by applicable ECTA s42–46, CPA, service type, customer status, actual commencement/performance, prior disclosures and other law; do NOT claim no refunds under all circumstances. ECTA s43 requires adequate pre-order information/review; noncompliance can carry a separate cancellation remedy. ECTA s44 may grant covered consumers seven days after service-agreement conclusion to cancel without reason, subject to statutory exclusions/actual fact; refunds within the applicable statutory period. ECTA s46 sets service-performance/default rights and remedies depending on agreement, statutory exclusions and notice. Cancellation and refund eligibility must be decided per lawful order facts, not arbitrarily denied as "digital content immediately delivered" without legal assessment.
Refund for duplicate capture, authorised reversal, service failure, incorrect amount, material misdescription or legally mandated cancellation follows actual PSP original-transaction linkage and audit. Use original payment method where legally/provider permitted; never invite users to send unverified replacement bank account details through WhatsApp/email to "process refunds". Communicate outcome, timing, evidence and complaint escalations. Chargebacks are not grounds to expose confidential evidence to an unrelated party without lawful processing.

## 7. Records, disputes and financial transparency
Issue order confirmation and proper receipt/invoice under SAR legal identity as applicable, not fictitious "DealScope (Pty) Ltd". Preserve accepted terms, price version, full tax, provider reference, merchant ID, paid/refunded amounts and customer transaction record. Service complaints go to a functioning DealScope support channel to be created and publicly disclosed; no support contact is represented as live until tested. No binding paid agreement can be turned on with missing supplier/price/phone/address/terms/refund/merchant identity disclosures.

## 8. Controls / acceptance gates
Legal counsel approves consumer/B2B applicability, cancellation and refund matrix; finance approves SAR merchant, tax/VAT and correct ledger; product verifies no-autoconvert, order review, idempotency and failed/duplicate refund; security verifies checkout/RLS, provider webhook and data privacy; Founder authorises published price list and operational launch. This file is NOT a live refund policy.
